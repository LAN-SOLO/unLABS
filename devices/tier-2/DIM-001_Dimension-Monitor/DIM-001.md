The Dimension Monitor is a lightweight quantum instrument for probing dimensional space, scanning for rift activity, and maintaining stability locks on detected anomalies. Equipped with halo monitoring and auto-calibration, the DIM-001 provides real-time dimensional telemetry as part of the Quantum tech tree's third tier of research.
