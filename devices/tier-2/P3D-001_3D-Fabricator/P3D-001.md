## Description

The 3D Fabricator is a heavy-class additive manufacturing device providing multi-material fabrication capabilities for the lab. Featuring automated bed leveling, nozzle calibration, extrusion control, layer tracking, and thermal management, the P3D-001 is the highest power-draw device in the Tools & Fabrication group at 60 E/s full load. It enables physical construction of lab components and crystal housings.
