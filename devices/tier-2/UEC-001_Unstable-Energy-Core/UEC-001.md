## Description

The Unstable Energy Core is the primary power generation device for the entire lab, converting real-time blockchain TPS (transactions per second) data into energy output for all connected equipment. It monitors network volatility, calculates energy tiers, and stabilizes field output. With a maximum output of 500 E/s and per-tier generation of 100 E/s, the UEC-001 is the foundational device in the Tech tree and the power backbone of every lab configuration.

## Terminal Command

```

```

**Aliases**: energycore, core, energy

### Subcommands

- `status` — Show device status
- `power` — Show power output and consumption
- `firmware` — Show firmware info
- `test` — Run diagnostics
- `reset` — Reset device to defaults
- `fold` — Collapse device panel
- `unfold` — Expand device panel
- `toggle` — Toggle device panel visibility
- `info` — Show device information
