## Description

The Handmade Synthesizer is a medium-class fabrication instrument specializing in slice synthesis and trait morphing for crystal manipulation. Equipped with multi-oscillator waveform generation and a filter bank, the HMS-001 provides the core synthesis capabilities needed for crystal slice processing. It features a resonance mode drawing up to 12 E/s for enhanced output fidelity.
