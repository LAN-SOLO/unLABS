## Description

The Explorer Drone is a heavy-class autonomous exploration unit capable of resource scanning, cargo hauling, and GPS-locked navigation. With IMU stabilization and a 2.4 km operational range, the EXD-001 is the primary field reconnaissance and resource gathering device within the Exploration tech tree. It operates at high power draw but delivers critical exploration data and material returns.
