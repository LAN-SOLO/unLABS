## Description

The Precision Laser is a heavy-class cutting and fabrication tool utilizing a diode array with optical focus calibration for high-precision operations. Equipped with power regulation and thermal protection systems, the LCT-001 delivers controlled energy output for crystal slicing and material processing. Its high full-load draw of 55 E/s is offset by extremely low idle and standby consumption.
