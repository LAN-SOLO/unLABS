## Description

Master power management system controlling all 30+ devices in the laboratory. The Power Management System is the central authority for energy distribution, device power states, fault detection, and emergency power protocols. It interfaces with every managed device to coordinate power budgets, handle load balancing, and enforce priority-based power allocation during constrained conditions.

## Firmware

No firmware file — device has no dedicated manager context with firmware export. Power management logic is handled at the system level within `PowerManager.tsx`.

## Power Specifications

Device operates as the master power controller with no self-reported power draw.

- **Status**: Always ONLINE

## Terminal Command

```
power [status|devices|grid|on|off|fault|emergency]
```

**Aliases**: `pwr`, `pwrmgmt`, `energy`

### Subcommands

- `status` — Show power system status and grid summary
- `devices` — List all devices and their power states
- `grid` — Display power grid topology and flow
- `on` — Power on a specified device
- `off` — Power off a specified device
- `fault` — Show power fault log and diagnostics
- `emergency` — Trigger emergency power protocol

## Device States

`online` (always)
