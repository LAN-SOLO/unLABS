## Description

Electromagnetic resource attraction device that generates calibrated magnetic fields to collect raw materials and resources from the surrounding environment. The Resource Magnet uses precision coil feedback, flux stabilization, and field calibration to optimize resource collection rates. Collected resources are deposited into the ATK-001 Abstractum Tank for storage.

- `reboot` — Restart magnet controller
