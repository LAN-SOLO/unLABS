# Transmissions: campaign strategy

The marketing restart (2026-10-03). The channels have been silent for a long
time. The campaign does not apologise for that silence. It treats the silence
as part of the story: the link to the lab was down, and now it is up.

The game itself says why. In the MCP chamber the log reads
`External contact: NOT ATTEMPTED. Status: WAITING.` The campaign is that
contact. Every post is a **transmission** from the lab, and the audience
receives it. Trailers are not "announcements"; they are signal.

## 0. Where the channel left off

The Telegram channel `_unstablecoins` (448 members, Discord pinned) already
speaks in a fixed form. Each post is a short glitched clip (pixelated
artefacts, RGB-split corridors, a wireframe boot GUI, a scanlined Jade
portrait) plus a status object in a code block, `//` comments and a closing
`[TAG]`:

- `ARTIFACT_REFLECTION`: `[ARTIFACTS_REVEALING_DEEPER_NATURE]`
- `LABORATORY_ENVIRONMENTS` (rgb_sync): `[MAINTAIN_ACTIVE_MONITORING_FOR_REVELATIONS]`
- `INTERFACE_RESTORATION` (06.06, boot sequence): `[INTERFACE_REVIVAL_IN_PROGRESS]`
- `JADE_QUOTE_DISCOVERED` (19 Feb): `[PHILOSOPHICAL_ARTIFACT_LOGGED]`
- **Last post, 23 April:** `RESTORATION_INITIATION`, module
  `COMMUNICATION_SUBSYSTEM`, reconstruction `SUCCESSFUL`, "Data pathways
  reopening for enhanced transmission capability."
  `[RESTORE_PROCESS_EXECUTING]`

The silence since April therefore already has a story: the restore process
was running. The campaign picks up exactly there. The restore is complete
and the link is up. We keep the established form (clip + status object), so
long-time members recognise the voice at once, and the new trailers are
the "enhanced transmission capability" that the last post promised.

## 1. Core message

> **The connection to the lab is finally established, and the story starts now.**

We never say this in plain words. The material shows it:

- The first frames are a link handshake. The text glitches, the signal drops
  out, and then it holds.
- The link quality rises over the first transmission until it reads
  `LINK STABLE`. After that the lab is "live".
- Every piece is numbered (`TRANSMISSION 01`, `02`, …), so it is clear that
  the story continues and that more will arrive.

## 2. Why glitches (and why that is strategic)

The \_unstable lab is unstable, and so is its communication. Glitches do
three jobs at once:

1. **Story.** A link that is being established is noisy. Noise means it is
   live and real, and it is happening now.
2. **Information control.** A glitch hides exactly what we do not want to
   show yet: unfinished rooms, mechanics that will change, anything
   spoilery. Nobody misses what a dropout swallows. It reads as intended.
3. **Room for change.** The game is still changing (skins, root lab, matrix
   chamber, the Damien arc). A feed that is "still stabilising" never
   promises a final look. Later transmissions can look better and that
   feels like progress, not like a broken promise.

Rules: glitches are short (2–10 frames) and sit on cuts or right before a
reveal. They never cover a full shot, and none of them is a strobe (no
flash cuts, keep the photosensitivity risk low). Glitch density follows the
story. The link improves through T01, stays clean in T02 while Damien's
signal is missing, and degrades again with depth in T03.

## 3. What we show and what we hold back

| Show (atmosphere)                                        | Hold back (substance)                                       |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| The lab waking: light, screens, voxels assembling        | How puzzles, crafting, power or root mode work              |
| Jade from behind, in silhouette, in profile              | Jade's face in full light (the hero is still being refined) |
| Damien as an absence: his desk, the mug, a veiled figure | Damien himself (he stays veiled until `damien_found`)       |
| Places: control room, MCP, data centre, cave             | Endings, the `kristall` secret, the 30 slices               |
| Numbers from the lore: 2,561 days, 847, 03:27            | Dates, platforms, prices, any "release on …"                |
| One hidden detail per transmission                       | Anything on-chain, ETH, mint or marketplace (legal gate)    |

There are no promises in any form: no release dates, no feature lists and
no "coming soon". The end card says `LINK ESTABLISHED` and the transmission
number. That is a status, not a promise.

## 4. The transmissions

| #   | Title            | Length | Link         | The question it leaves                    |
| --- | ---------------- | ------ | ------------ | ----------------------------------------- |
| 00  | Signal           | 15 s   | 4 → 100 %    | What just answered?                       |
| 01  | Still Running    | 66 s   | 12 → 100 %   | Who kept the lab running for 2,561 days?  |
| 02  | Where Is Damien? | 70 s   | 100 % · D: — | Where did he go, and who is on that feed? |
| 03  | 847 Metres       | 75 s   | 100 → 31 %   | What is down there, and why 847?          |

- **00 Signal** is the comeback post: black, static, a handshake that fails
  twice and then holds, half a second of the lab, then `LINK ESTABLISHED`.
  It is cut from T01 material and carries no new information. Its only job
  is to say "we are back" without saying it.
- **01** shows the lab waking. The link reaches 100 % on the reveal of the
  lit control room.
- **02** keeps the lab link stable, but the search for D. Fridge returns
  `NO SIGNAL`. The one glitch in this trailer is the CCTV frame with the
  veiled figure.
- **03** goes down the shaft. The deeper the camera goes, the weaker the
  link. At 847 m it nearly breaks, and the cave is the clearest image in the
  trailer, as if something down there is boosting the signal.

## 5. Hidden details (community hooks)

Every transmission hides one thing that is real game data. We never confirm
or explain them; the community finds them.

- **00:** the failed handshakes are timed in 847 ms steps.
- **01:** the data-centre racks blink at 847 ms.
- **02:** the radio-room sconce pulses the chalk morse line (L-O-V-W).
- **03:** the depth counter, and the timestamp on the CCTV feed in 02
  (03:12:44, the minute of the tertiary shutdown log).

If people decode something, we reply in character (as the MCP: dry, a
little reluctant). We never confirm the answer.

## 6. Rollout

The rhythm is one transmission per week. Between them, the channels carry
small "intercepts" that are cheap to make and build the habit:

| Week | Main post                                                                        | Intercepts (2–3 per week)                                                           |
| ---- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 0    | **00 Signal** (all channels)                                                     | A static image `LINK ESTABLISHED`; the profile banner switches to the glitched logo |
| 1    | **01 Still Running**                                                             | Stills from T01 with a line from the MCP; a 9:16 cut-down of the bot waking         |
| 2    | **02 Where Is Damien?**                                                          | The cold mug (one still, one line); the CCTV frame as a single image                |
| 3    | **03 847 Metres**                                                                | The depth counter as a loop; the cave still                                         |
| 4+   | Transmissions continue as the game grows (devlog-in-character, new rooms, skins) |                                                                                     |

- **Formats:** 16:9 for YouTube, X and Discord. 9:16 for Reels, TikTok and
  Shorts (its own camera per shot, not a crop). The first second must work
  without sound: the handshake text carries it.
- **Voice:** every caption continues the channel's status-object form
  (section 9). No marketing copy, no exclamation marks, no hype words.
  On X / TikTok, where code blocks do not render, use the one-line form
  `TX 01 // LINK 100% // the lab never slept.`
- **Pinned:** the latest transmission is always pinned, so a newcomer lands
  on the newest "signal".

## 7. Guardrails

- Everything on screen is the game's own data: rooms, devices, music and
  sound. Nothing is faked or shows something the game does not have.
- Voxels only (docs/CLARITY.md). Jade is the only realistic figure.
- Damien is only ever veiled.
- No on-chain content until the legal review is done.
- No photosensitive strobing. Glitch frames stay short and are not flashes.

## 8. Production

The trailers are built by the pipeline in docs/TRAILERS.md. The glitch
layer is applied in `scripts/trailer/assemble.mjs` from the `glitch` cues in
`timeline.mjs`, so the density can be tuned without re-rendering. The link
HUD (`LINK nn%`) is rendered in Blender as voxel text, like every other
text.
